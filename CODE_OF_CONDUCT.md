# Code of Conduct

Velox is built by volunteers from many backgrounds. We want taking part to be a good experience
for everyone, whatever their level of experience, identity or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Be patient with newcomers and help them find their way.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and chat, and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the project page.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants, in response to behavior they judge inappropriate. Maintainers who do not
follow this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
